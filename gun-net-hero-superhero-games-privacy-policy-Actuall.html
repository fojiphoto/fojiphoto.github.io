Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Factorial Studio Private Limited (“Factorial Studio”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use:

  • Our websites, and
  • Our mobile games and apps, including but not limited to titles published under our developer accounts (collectively, the “Apps” ).

By installing, accessing, or using any of our Apps, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Types of Data We Collect

1.1 Non-Personal Data

We may collect data that does not identify you personally , such as:

  • Gameplay statistics and actions
  • In-game events and progress
  • Device type, OS version, language, and general location (country/region)
  • App version and performance data (crashes, errors, loading time, etc.)

This data is mainly used for analytics, optimization, and improving user experience.

1.2 Personal Data

“Personal Data” means information that can identify you directly or indirectly (for example, an email address or an advertising identifier).

Depending on the App and how you use it, we may collect:

(a) Device & Usage Information (automatically collected)

  • Device model, operating system and version
  • IP address
  • Language and country-level location
  • Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • In-game actions, session length, features used
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks, installs from ads)

(b) Identifiers and Account Data (if applicable)

  • Username or display name
  • Email address (if you register, sign up for newsletters, or contact us)
  • A randomly generated user ID for your game profile

(c) Customer Support

When you contact us (e.g., via email or in-App support), we may process:

  • Your name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Details of your request and any other information you voluntarily provide

(d) In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

  • Purchase history (type of item or subscription, date, country)
  • We do not receive or store your full payment card details. All payments are processed by Google Play , Apple App Store , or other authorized payment providers, according to their own terms and privacy policies.

(e) Microphone and Camera (only in some Apps)

Some Apps may request access to the microphone or camera for features such as:

  • Voice chat between players
  • Augmented Reality (AR) features
  • In-game recording or sharing gameplay

Where these features exist:

  • They are optional and require your device-level permission.
  • We do not store or share your audio/video content unless clearly stated and you consent (e.g., when you upload/share content yourself).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  1. To operate and provide the Apps Save and sync your game progress Maintain your user profile and allow play across multiple devices Support in-App features such as leaderboards, events, and in-game rewards
  2. To improve and personalize the Apps Analyze gameplay and user behavior Fix bugs, crashes, and technical issues Optimize difficulty, features, and content Show relevant in-game content or offers
  3. Advertising & User Acquisition Show ads in our Apps (including rewarded ads, banner ads, and interstitial ads) Measure performance of marketing campaigns Limit how often you see the same ad
  4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions Enable purchase and delivery of in-App items Manage “No Ads” purchases (where available) Manage subscriptions, including free trials and renewals
  5. Communication Respond to support requests Send important notices about changes to the Apps or this Privacy Policy Send promotional emails if you have given consent (where required by law)
  6. Legal & Security Prevent fraud and abuse Enforce our Terms of Use Comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Bases (for EEA/UK Users)

Where applicable (for example, if you are located in the EEA or UK), we process your Personal Data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: To provide you with the App and related services.
  • Legitimate interests: To improve the Apps, prevent fraud, and secure our systems.
  • Consent: For personalized ads or marketing emails where required.
  • Legal obligation: Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws.

You may withdraw your consent at any time via your device settings (for ads) or by contacting us.

We may share your information in the following limited situations:

  1. Service Providers With trusted third parties that help us operate the Apps, such as: Cloud hosting providers Analytics and attribution partners Customer support tools Advertising partners (as described above)
  2. Legal Requirements & Protection Where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to respond to lawful requests by public authorities, or to protect our rights, users, or others.

6.1 How to Delete Your Game Data

If you no longer want us to process data related to our games, you can:

  1. Uninstall the game from your device This removes local game data (such as progress stored on the device) and stops the game from collecting new data.
  2. Reset or limit your Advertising ID on your device On Android: go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID and/or Opt out of Ads Personalization On iOS: go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and adjust tracking preferences
  3. Contact us to request deletion of your data Email us at contact@factorialstudio.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request – [Game Name]” Include your country, platform (Google Play / iOS), and device type. Where technically possible, we will delete or anonymize data linked to your device or identifiers and, if applicable, forward the request to certain third-party providers (ads/analytics) as required by their policies.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to:

When data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it. You may also request deletion of your Personal Data (see “Your Rights” below).

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Ask us to delete your Personal Data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict certain processing.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.

9. Managing Ads & Tracking

You can control personalized advertising and tracking in several ways:

  • Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or similar, depending on OS version).
  • iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking , and adjust app permissions.
  • In-App Settings (if available): Some Apps may include a privacy or settings section where you can change ad or analytics preferences.

Note: You will still see ads after opting out, but they may be less relevant.
