What We Create

A complete art department — from first concept sketch to fully rigged, animated, engine-integrated assets.

Concept Art

Mood boards, key art, and visual exploration that define the look and tone of your game before production begins.

2D Art & Illustration

Sprites, backgrounds, icons, and illustrations — from clean vector to richly painted, in any 2D style.

3D Modeling

Game-ready 3D models with clean topology, optimized for real-time performance across all platforms.

Character Design

Memorable heroes, enemies, and NPCs — designed for personality, readability, and animation from the start.

Environment Art

Worlds, levels, and props that feel alive — modular, optimized, and built to support gameplay.

Rigging & Animation

Skeletal rigs and fluid animation — characters, creatures, and cinematics that move with weight and life.

VFX & Shaders

Particles, spells, impacts, and custom shaders that add juice and polish without tanking performance.

UI Art

Menus, HUDs, and icon sets that look great and stay consistent across every screen and resolution.

Marketing / Key Art

Store creatives, splash art, and promo visuals built to make your game stand out and convert.

Process

How We Create

A clear art pipeline — direction first, then exploration, production, and integration — so the result fits your game perfectly.

01

Art Direction & Style

We define the visual language — references, palette, and style guide — so every asset stays cohesive and on-brand.

02

Concept & Exploration

Rapid sketches and concepts to explore options early and lock the direction before committing to final production.

03

Production

Full asset creation — modeling, 2D, rigging, and animation — built to spec with clean, optimized, game-ready output.

04

Texturing & Lighting

PBR texturing, materials, and lighting passes that give assets depth, mood, and a finished, professional look.

05

Engine Integration

We deliver and integrate assets directly into Unity or Unreal — correctly scaled, optimized, and in-engine ready.

06

Review & Polish

Iteration rounds and a final polish pass to make sure every asset hits the bar before it ships.
