The Basics

What ASO Actually Is

App Store Optimization is the process of getting your app to rank higher in store search — through keyword strategy, visual optimization, and metadata work.

With millions of apps competing, the store algorithms decide who gets discovered. They change constantly, and what worked last year often doesn't now.

It isn't a one-time fix. It's continuous testing against a moving target.

  • Keyword research & optimization — finding high-performing, relevant terms that maximize reach
  • Title & description enhancement — keyword-rich copy that ranks and still reads like a human wrote it
  • Visual asset optimization — icons, screenshots and videos A/B tested for conversion
  • Ratings & review strategy — improving and managing reputation, which feeds ranking
  • Competitive analysis — benchmarking against who's beating you and why
  • Analytics & tracking — measuring and adjusting rather than setting and forgetting

FAQ

Straight Answers

How long until ASO shows results?

Keyword changes typically take a few days to a couple of weeks to settle in ranking. Conversion improvements from creative tests show faster. Meaningful organic growth is usually a matter of months, not days.

Do you need access to our store console?

For implementation and A/B testing, yes. For the initial audit, no — the public listing tells us most of what we need.

Is ASO worth it if we're already running paid UA?

Especially then. Paid campaigns push ranking up; ASO makes sure that ranking converts. Without it you're renting visibility instead of building it.

Can you help with ratings?

Yes — through legitimate in-app prompt timing and review response strategy. We don't buy reviews; it violates store policy and gets apps removed.

Do you handle localization?

Store listings can be localized per market, and it's often the single highest-ROI ASO change for apps with international reach. Ask us about it in the audit.
