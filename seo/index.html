FAQ

Straight Answers

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can show within weeks. Competitive rankings usually take 3-6 months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling something else.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and nobody credible does — Google's algorithm isn't ours to control. We commit to the work, the reporting, and honest assessment of what's achievable in your niche.

Do you build the site too?

We can. SEO handled by the same team that builds the site means no handoff gaps — see Web Development. SEO best practice goes in from the first line of code rather than being retrofitted.

What about backlinks?

Earned outreach to relevant sites. We don't buy links — it works briefly then earns a penalty that takes far longer to recover from.

Can you fix a traffic drop?

Usually we can at least diagnose it — algorithm update, technical regression after a redesign, lost backlinks, or cannibalisation. That's what the free audit is for.
