Process

How We Grow It

A proven growth loop — validate, optimize, scale — repeated until your game is a profitable, charting title.

01

Market & Store Audit

We analyze your game, category, competitors, and current funnel to find the biggest growth levers before spending a rupee.

02

Store Optimization (ASO)

We rebuild the store presence — keywords, icon, screenshots, video — and A/B test to lift organic installs and conversion.

03

Soft Launch & Validation

Geo soft-launch to measure retention, monetization, and economy — we only scale what the data proves works.

04

UA Campaign Scaling

We launch and optimize paid campaigns across networks, tuning creatives and bids toward target CPI and ROAS.

05

Monetization Tuning

Ad placement, IAP, and rewarded-video balance tuned to lift ARPDAU while protecting the player experience.

06

Global Scale & Live-Ops

Confident worldwide rollout backed by events, content, and continuous experimentation to keep growth compounding.
