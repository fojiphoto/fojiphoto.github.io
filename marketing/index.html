FAQ

Straight Answers

What's the minimum ad budget worth starting with?

Below roughly $1,000/month there usually isn't enough data for the platforms to optimize, so you're paying for learning you can't act on. We'll tell you honestly if you're better off waiting.

Do you need access to our ad accounts?

Yes, or we set them up for you. Accounts stay in your name and under your ownership — you keep the history and the data if we ever stop working together.

Do you handle creatives too?

We provide ad copy and creative direction, and our in-house design team can produce the assets. See Design.

How does this connect to ASO and UA?

Closely. Paid installs and organic store visibility feed each other — see ASO and UA Services.

How soon do we see results?

Campaigns need a learning period before performance stabilises — typically a few weeks. Anyone promising immediate ROAS is guessing.
