FAQ

Straight Answers

Do I have to send you my project files?

No. We test the public build — the same one your players download. Source access only matters later, if you hire us for engine-level work.

How long does it take?

Usually a few working days. If your game is live and we can download it immediately, often sooner.

How much of my game will you actually test?

A few hours' worth — enough to cover the opening, core loop, and the paths players hit most. We'll tell you exactly what we skipped rather than implying we covered everything.

What if you don't find anything?

Then you get a short report saying so, and that's genuinely useful information. It happens, and we won't invent findings to look busy.

Will I get chased by a sales team?

You'll get the report and one follow-up asking if it was useful. That's it. We don't run outreach sequences on people who did us the favour of replying.

Is my unreleased game eligible?

Not through this offer — we only teardown builds that are already public. For pre-release work we'd sign an NDA and scope it properly as a paid engagement.

Who actually does the testing?

Our own QA engineers in Lahore — not a crowdsourced tester pool. The person who writes your report is the person who played your game.
