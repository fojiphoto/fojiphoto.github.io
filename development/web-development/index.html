FAQ

Straight Answers

WordPress or custom build?

WordPress when you need your team editing content daily. Custom when performance, unusual functionality or a specific experience matters more. We'll recommend based on who maintains it after launch.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

We can set both up, or deploy to infrastructure you already have.

Will it be SEO-ready?

Structurally yes — speed, semantics, metadata and indexing. Ongoing ranking work is a separate service: SEO.

Can you redesign without losing our rankings?

Yes, and it needs care — redirect mapping, preserved URL structure and content parity. Redesigns done carelessly are one of the most common causes of sudden traffic loss.

Do you provide the design too?

Yes — design and development in one team. See Design.
