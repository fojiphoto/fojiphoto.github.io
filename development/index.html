FAQ

Straight Answers

How do you price projects?

Three ways: fixed scope for defined projects, monthly dedicated team for ongoing work, or hourly time-and-materials where requirements are still moving.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. That's written into the contract before work starts.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. We'd start with a code and architecture review so we can give you an honest assessment rather than a hopeful one — sometimes the honest answer is that parts need rebuilding.

What engines and stacks do you use?

Unity and Unreal for games; Swift, Kotlin, Flutter and React Native for apps; modern web stacks and WordPress for sites. We pick per project, not per habit.

What happens after launch?

Ongoing support for updates, new content and live-ops. Most clients continue with us on a monthly basis after the initial build.
