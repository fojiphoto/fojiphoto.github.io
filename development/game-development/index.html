FAQ

Straight Answers

What does a game cost to build?

It depends entirely on scope, art volume and platforms. A 2D casual title and a 3D mid-core game differ by an order of magnitude. Send the brief and we'll give you a real number.

How long does it take?

Small casual titles can ship in a few months; larger projects take considerably longer. We give a milestone plan up front rather than one big deadline.

Do we own the game?

Yes — code, art and IP. Written into the contract before work begins.

Can you finish a game someone else started?

Often yes, after a code review. We'll tell you honestly if inheriting it costs more than rebuilding the weak parts.

Can you also market it?

Yes — see UA, ASO and Monetization.
