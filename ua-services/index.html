FAQ

Straight Answers

Is my app ready for paid UA?

Only if retention holds. Paid UA amplifies whatever your product already does — if users leave on day one, UA just makes you lose money faster. We'll tell you if that's the case.

What CPI should I expect?

It varies enormously by genre, geo and platform. Anyone quoting a CPI before seeing your app is guessing. We give a range after looking at your category and competitors.

Do we keep ownership of the ad accounts?

Yes. Accounts stay in your name with your billing, so you keep all campaign history and learning data.

How is this different from your Marketing service?

Marketing is the broader Google Ads and Meta offering including web and brand. UA is specifically mobile app and game install growth with LTV modelling.

Do you handle iOS post-ATT tracking limits?

Yes — SKAdNetwork-aware campaign structure and modelled measurement, since deterministic per-user attribution isn't available on iOS any more.
