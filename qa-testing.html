Methodology

How We Test

No emulator-only guesswork. We test live, on the engine and on real hardware — the same conditions your players are in.

01

Live Unity & Unreal Testing

We test live inside the engine — and for console titles, with the devkit physically attached — watching profiler data and errors in real time.

02

Real-Device Lab

50+ real iOS & Android devices, flagship to genuine low-end. Touch, battery & thermal only trust real hardware.

03

Crash, ANR & Logs

We reproduce issues, pull full logs & stack traces, and confirm each in logs before it reaches your team.

04

Thermal & Low-End Profiling

Extended sessions track heating, throttling & battery drain; low-end devices tested separately.

05

Aggressive & Edge-Case

Input spamming, interruptions, network drops & impossible states — we test the way an exploit would.

06

Reporting & Retest

Severity, repro, screenshots, device & logs — delivered in 24 hours. We retest fixes to confirm closure.
