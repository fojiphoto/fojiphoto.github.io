What We Test

Every build is put through a full battery of test disciplines — far beyond "does it launch". Here's what we check, on every pass.

Functional Testing

Every mechanic, menu, transition, and edge case verified against the design spec — making sure the game does exactly what it should, everywhere it should.

Performance & FPS

Frame-rate stability, load times, memory usage, and frame drops profiled across device tiers — from flagship to budget hardware.

Crash & ANR Analysis

We hunt crashes and ANRs (Application Not Responding), capture full stack traces and logs, and pinpoint the exact reproduction path so devs can fix fast.

Low-End Device Testing

If your game struggles on a low-RAM, older-chipset phone, you lose players. We test on genuine low-end devices to expose lag, stutter, and memory crashes.

Thermal / Heating Tests

We measure how hot each device runs during extended play, identify which devices overheat and throttle, and flag the scenarios that drain battery or trigger thermal cut-offs.

Aggressive / Stress Testing

Rapid input spamming, rotation abuse, interruptions (calls, notifications), connection drops, and impossible state combinations — we deliberately try to break it.

Compatibility Testing

Across OS versions, screen sizes, aspect ratios, resolutions, and hardware combinations — making sure the experience holds on every target device.

Log Verification

We capture and read engine & device logs (logcat, console output, crash dumps) to verify behaviour, catch silent errors, and confirm fixes actually landed.

Compliance / Cert

Platform submission checks for the App Store, Google Play, and console first-party (Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo) requirements — so you pass cert the first time.

Methodology

How We Test

No emulator-only guesswork. Our QA engineers test live, on the engine and on real hardware — the same conditions your players are in.

01

Live Testing on Unity & Unreal

We test live inside the engine with the build connected — and for console titles, with the devkit/console physically attached. This lets us watch behaviour, profiler data, and errors in real time as they happen, not after the fact.

02

Real-Device Lab — Not Emulators

A lab of 50+ real iOS & Android devices spanning flagship to genuine low-end hardware. Touch response, sensors, battery, and thermal behaviour can only be trusted on real devices.

03

Crash, ANR & Log Verification

We reproduce crashes and ANRs, pull the full logs and stack traces, and verify them against the build. Every reported issue is confirmed in logs before it reaches your dev team — no noise, no false positives.

04

Thermal & Low-End Profiling

We run extended sessions to track device heating, thermal throttling, and battery drain — and identify exactly which devices overheat. Low-end devices are tested separately to catch the lag and memory crashes flagships hide.

05

Aggressive & Edge-Case Passes

Beyond the happy path: input spamming, rapid scene switching, interruptions, network drops, and impossible state combinations. We test the way a determined player — or an exploit — would deliberately try to break it.

06

Detailed Reporting & Retest

Each issue is logged with severity, repro steps, screenshots/video, device info, and logs — delivered within 24 hours. Once fixed, we retest and confirm closure so nothing slips back in.

Deliverables

Bug Reports You Can Act On

  • Severity & priority — every issue triaged (Critical / High / Medium / Low) so you fix what matters first.

  • Device & build info — model, OS, build number, and conditions captured automatically.
