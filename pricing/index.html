Straight Talk

What Actually Drives the Number

If you want to estimate before talking to us, these are the levers. They matter far more than the hourly rate.

Art Volume

On games this is usually the single biggest line — and the most underestimated. Fifty unique items with multiple states is a different project from ten.

Platform Count

Each additional platform adds build, test and certification work. Two platforms is not twice one, but it's nowhere near the same either.

Whether It Already Exists

Porting or extending something built is usually far cheaper than building new. Inheriting a badly-built codebase sometimes isn't — we'll review and tell you honestly.

How Firm the Scope Is

A locked scope prices lowest. Genuinely moving requirements are better on T&M — fixed-price with a moving scope just means everyone argues later.

Timeline Pressure

Compressing a schedule means more people in parallel, which costs more than the same work done sequentially. Sometimes worth it, often not.

Localization & Compliance

Multi-language support, console certification and store policy work are real line items that teams routinely forget to budget for.

FAQ

Straight Answers

Why don't you list prices?

Because a published rate tells you almost nothing about what your project costs. The scope drives the number, not the rate. Two studios quoting the same hourly rate can differ 3× on the same brief.

How fast do I get a quote?

Usually within two working days of us understanding the scope. Larger projects may need a short call first.

Is there a minimum engagement?

For dedicated team work, one month. For fixed-scope projects it depends on the work — small QA passes and audits can be genuinely small.

What if my budget is smaller than the scope?

We'll tell you, and usually propose a smaller first phase that still delivers something real. We'd rather scope down than pad an estimate and disappoint you later.

Who owns what we pay for?

You do — code, art and IP, written into the contract before work starts.

How do payments work?

Milestone-based for fixed scope, monthly in advance for dedicated teams, monthly in arrears for T&M. Agreed in writing before anything begins.
