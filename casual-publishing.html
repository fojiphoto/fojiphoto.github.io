1. Object

These terms and conditions (the “Submission T&Cs” ) govern the submission of mobile games to Factorial Studio Private Limited (“ Factorial Studio ”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) under our Casual Publishing program .

2. Submission

  • Any individual or entity that has developed a mobile game (published or unpublished) may submit a game to Factorial Studio for review and consideration under our Casual Publishing program.

The Participating Studio understands and agrees that:

  • A submission does not create any obligation on Factorial Studio to: Review the game; Provide feedback; or Enter into any agreement or business relationship.
  • Once a game is submitted, Factorial Studio may or may not contact the Participating Studio for further discussion, at its sole discretion.

3. Reference (Referral)

If the Submission Form includes the details of a third-party individual or entity who introduced the Participating Studio to Factorial Studio’s Casual Publishing program (the “Reference” ), then:

  • Factorial Studio may, at its sole discretion , offer a referral reward to the Reference if: The Participating Studio later signs a publishing or cooperation agreement with Factorial Studio for a submitted game; and That game reaches a specific performance or revenue milestone defined by Factorial Studio in the applicable referral program at that time (the “Referral Reward” ).
  • Any Referral Reward (if applicable) will: Only be payable once per game , and Only be payable to one Reference identified and validated by Factorial Studio.
  • The Reference must not : Have a direct ownership interest in the Participating Studio; or Be a direct employee, partner, or shareholder of the Participating Studio.

Referral programs, thresholds, and amounts may be changed, suspended, or discontinued by Factorial Studio at any time, and no entitlement exists unless and until a separate referral confirmation is agreed in writing by Factorial Studio.
