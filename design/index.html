FAQ

Straight Answers

Can you match an existing art style?

Yes — that's most of the game art work we do. Send references or existing assets and we'll produce a test piece before committing to volume.

What formats do you deliver in?

Whatever your pipeline needs — layered source files, engine-ready sprites and atlases, Figma, or export presets you specify.

Do you design store screenshots for ASO?

Yes, and we design them as testable variants rather than a single set — which is how you find out which one actually converts. See ASO.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are agreed in the scope up front, so there's no argument about it later.

Can you design and build?

Yes — see Development. Same team means the design is buildable by construction.
