FAQ

Straight Answers

Will more ads hurt my retention?

Badly placed ones will. That's the whole craft — frequency capping, placement at natural break points, and rewarded formats the player chooses. We measure retention alongside revenue, not instead of it.

Do you need code access?

For SDK integration and mediation setup, yes — under NDA. For the initial revenue review, no.

Which ad networks do you work with?

The major ones including AppLovin, Mintegral, AdMob, Unity Ads and Meta, plus mediation so they compete for each impression rather than one network setting the price.

Ads or IAP — which should we use?

Usually both, weighted by genre and geo. Ads monetize the 95%+ who never pay; IAP monetizes the few who pay a lot. Choosing only one leaves money on the table.

How fast do results show?

Mediation and placement changes can move ARPDAU within weeks. Pricing and subscription tests need longer to reach significance.
